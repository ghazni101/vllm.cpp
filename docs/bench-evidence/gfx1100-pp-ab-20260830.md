## Method

`vllm-bench --model <gguf> --input-len L --output-len 1 --num-prompts 5 --concurrency 1 --seed 42 --temperature 0`

Two consecutive runs per prompt length. Median TTFT is the median of the two runs' median TTFT values. Prefill throughput is the median of the two runs' prefill throughput.
